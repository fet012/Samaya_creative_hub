The Studio

A creative hub, not just a studio.

Samaya Creative Hub is a photo and video studio in Benin City. Samaya is a full production space recording, mixing, content creation and photography under one roof, run by people who care about the work as much as you do.

Whether you're booking thirty minutes for a portrait or a full day for a shoot, the space is designed to give your work room to happen properly no rush, no clutter, just the right backdrop and the right hands.
