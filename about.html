About Samaya

Samaya means "time." We built a place worth spending it in.

Our Story

Built around a single idea: time well spent.

Samaya Creative Hub started with a simple question, what would a studio look like if it treated every session as time worth protecting? Not a room you rush in and out of, but a space designed the way a good clock is designed: with intention, in every part.

How We Work

What you can expect from a session here.

01

Every room, considered

Each of our eight rooms is styled with real furniture, art and lighting — nothing thrown together, nothing generic.

02

Your time, protected

Sessions start on time and run on your schedule. We ask you to arrive early so the clock works in your favor, not against it.

03

Craft over shortcuts

From mixing to lighting, our team takes the extra pass. We'd rather get it right than get it done fast.

04

Your work stays yours

What you create in the space belongs to you. We're here to support the work, not to own it.
