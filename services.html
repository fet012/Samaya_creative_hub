Services & Rooms

Everything the work needs, in one building.

Six creative services and eight designed rooms — booked by the hour or the half hour, and built around whatever you're making.

01

Music Production

Beat-making, arrangement and full song production, worked through with an engineer who understands the sound you're going for — from first idea to a finished, release-ready track.

02

Photography

Portrait, editorial, product and brand photography across eight styled rooms, each with a different backdrop and light quality — or a full white cyclorama when you need a clean canvas.

03

Videography

Interviews, music videos, brand films and short-form content, shot and lit with proper equipment and an eye for the story you're actually trying to tell.

04

Content Creation

Reels, campaign shoots and social-ready content produced end to end — concept, styling, shooting and a first edit, sized for the platforms you actually post to.

05

Mixing & Mastering

Balanced mixes and loud, clean masters that translate across systems — from a single, focused revision to full-project mixing for an EP or album.

06

Recording Studio

A treated, acoustically-controlled room for vocals, instrumentals and voiceover, with an engineer on hand to keep the session moving.
